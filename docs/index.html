A little about me

Learning to represent,
reason, and act.

I'm Semen, a research engineer interested in how learned representations of the world can support reasoning, planning, and decision-making.

I currently lead tool-use post-training for GigaChat Omni at Sber, working on cold-start data, evaluations, and reinforcement learning. Previously, I started the team's VLM effort at Yandex Autonomous Vehicles, taking models from raw sensor data to on-vehicle testing.

I'm completing an M.Sc. in Deep Learning and Generative Artificial Intelligence at ITMO University, where I also studied Applied Mathematics and Computer Science.

Research

My interests sit at the intersection of world models, self-supervised representation learning, latent dynamics, and model-based reinforcement learning.

Predictive representationsResearch prototype · Yandex

Planning in latent space

I explored JEPA-based trajectory planning through sequential prediction of future latent states. A compact model with a ViT-B backbone and a roughly 20M-parameter latent predictor outperformed a substantially larger autoregressive planning baseline on selected driving scenarios.

Multimodal reasoningOngoing · ITMO & GigaChat

Allocating computation to reasoning

I'm studying variable-length latent and textual reasoning in vision–language models. I implemented up-front budget prediction and learned stop heads as alternative ways to control reasoning length.

I reproduced LVR, HyLaR, and LUT; current experiments use Qwen3.5 and Qwen3.8 (27B).

Data & perceptionBachelor's thesis · 2025

Finding data for rare road objects

I built a VLM-based pipeline for mining perception errors and selecting examples for detector fine-tuning, evaluating Yandex VLM, Qwen2.5-VL, and InternVL2.5 on 50,000 images. For the object-counting component, prompt tuning reduced MSE by 31% (15.55 → 10.73) relative to the same 35B baseline without adaptation.
